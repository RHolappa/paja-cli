# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, give actionable feedback, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and official project communication.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should consider severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people. Reporters' identities and private details should be shared only as needed to handle the report.

Report concerns privately to the designated project conduct contact. Before public launch, maintainers must add a monitored contact to [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md). Until one is listed, request a private contact without posting the incident or personal information in a public issue. Vulnerabilities follow [Security](SECURITY.md).

To appeal a decision, ask for review by another designated Innostium Oy representative where available. Good-faith reports and participation in a review must not lead to retaliation.
